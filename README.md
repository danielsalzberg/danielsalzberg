# Daniel Salzberg

**VP of Operations at BioMech Health · Founder, Salzberg & Associates, LLC · CPHIMS · MBA, East Carolina University · Richmond, Virginia**

Healthcare technology and operations executive in Richmond, Virginia, with about 25 years of experience turning clinical needs into technology that works in the real world. My focus is practical: start with the workflow, measure what matters, and make the right thing easy for clinicians to do.

### What I work on
- **Healthcare IT strategy:** enterprise systems, integration, and technology roadmaps
- **Population health and value-based care:** ACO technology, data, and outcomes measurement
- **Clinical transformation:** EMR deployment (including Epic integration) and health information exchange
- **Digital health operations:** remote monitoring and objective movement measurement

### Experience
- **BioMech Health**, VP of Operations (2025–present)
- **Salzberg & Associates, LLC**, Founder & Principal Consultant (2016–2025)
- **Strategic Solutions of Virginia**, Director of Healthcare Innovations (2018–2020)
- **Bon Secours Health System**, Administrative Director of Information Services (2006–2015)
