# Daniel Salzberg

**VP of Operations at BioMech Health · Founder, Salzberg & Associates, LLC · CPHIMS · MBA, East Carolina University**

Healthcare technology and operations executive with more than 20 years of experience turning clinical needs into technology that works in the real world. My focus is practical: start with the workflow, measure what matters, and make the right thing easy for clinicians to do.

- **Population health and value-based care:** ACO technology, data, and outcomes measurement
- **Clinical transformation:** EMR deployment (including Epic integration) and health information exchange
- **Digital health operations:** remote monitoring and objective movement measurement
